# Packaging Workflow

## Original Application

Design Calculation
↓

Physical Input

Purpose:
Validate estimation formulas.

---

## New Workflow

Existing Design
↓

Clone

↓

Modify

↓

Proposed Design

↓

Compare

Outputs:

- Packaging Weight
- Material CO₂
- Transport CO₂
- Total CO₂
- Sustainability Improvement

---

## Existing Design

Represents today's production packaging.

Input method:
- Measured if available.
- Estimated otherwise.

---

## Proposed Design

Starts as a clone of Existing Design.

Modify only the fields being redesigned.

Usually estimated.

May be measured if a prototype exists.

---

## Comparison

Existing is always the baseline.

Proposed is the candidate.

Show

- Absolute CO₂ reduction
- Percentage reduction
- Weight reduction

Do not compare simply to determine which is lower.

The purpose is to justify replacing the existing packaging.

---

## Out of Scope

- Cost calculations
- Freight cost
- Material price

These are future enhancements.
